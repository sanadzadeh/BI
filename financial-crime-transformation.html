Annual alert workload, handling time, triage effectiveness and change costs are fictional assumptions. The model assumes 30 minutes per alert before intervention. Accuracy influences the assisted workflow but cannot substitute for a validated false-negative assessment. Coverage scores represent illustrative scenario assumptions, not estimated regulatory control effectiveness. No production systems or customer records are used.
All client details, costs, data, outcomes, solution scores and recommendations are fictional. This is an analytical showcase of a proposed consulting approach, not a claim of completed client engagements or production AI deployment.
