Decision pathway

A simple structure for moving from demand intake to an auditable portfolio decision.

1. IntakeCapture request, outcome, timing, cost, dependencies and required evidence.
→
2. ValidateCheck completeness, financial assumptions, supplier data and capacity.
→
3. ModelTest OPEX/CAPEX, run-rate, recoveries, benefits and scenarios.
→
4. PrioritiseCompare value, urgency, constraints and portfolio fit.
→
5. GovernRecord decision, actions, owner, forecast impact and follow-up evidence.
