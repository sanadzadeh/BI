Validation pathway

From decision question to issued insight

QuestionDefine the decision and the measure required.
SourceMap system fields, ownership and grain.
RulesApply definitions, joins and business logic.
ValidateReconcile totals, exceptions and movements.
IssuePublish result with caveats and evidence.
Exception detected

Unexpected movement triggers a source and definition check before commentary is written.

Root cause confirmed

Different identifiers across systems explain duplicate records and inflated counts.

Rule corrected

Join logic and validation control are updated, then prior periods are re-tested.

Traceable

Every reported measure can be linked back to a source, definition and transformation rule.

Comparable

Periods use consistent logic so movement reflects the portfolio, not a reporting change.

Explainable

Known limitations are visible and the forum can see what the evidence does and does not support.
