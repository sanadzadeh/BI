      Model assumptions

      • Starting active membership: 200,000; base retention: 91.4%.
      • Baseline annual new members: 18,000; annual cost per member: $320.
      • Whole initiative funding, with costs charged against one three year envelope.
      • Initiative effects scale with the outcome realisation control.

      Validation needed

      • Reconcile active member definitions and opening balances.
      • Confirm acquisition and exit cohorts; avoid double counting.
      • Validate the cost allocation basis and service measure definitions.
      • Test whether forecast improvements can be delivered within capacity.
