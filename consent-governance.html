Executive view

Make consent decisions traceable across systems and channels

An illustrative framework for turning privacy and preference requirements into governed business rules, process flows, data mappings, validation controls and implementation decisions.

Design principle

A consent capability should make it clear who consented to what, when, through which channel, under which policy, and how that decision propagates across dependent systems.

Data & integration view
  • Authoritative consent record and ownership defined.
  • Source-to-target mappings documented for each system.
  • Rules distinguish capture, update, expiry and withdrawal.
  • Exceptions and reconciliation points identified.
  • Reporting exposes stale, missing or conflicting states.
  • Change impacts are assessed before release.

Outcome

Illustrative framework The aim is a consent capability where business requirements, system behaviour, data state and test evidence remain connected, reducing ambiguity during delivery and making future regulatory or product change easier to assess.
