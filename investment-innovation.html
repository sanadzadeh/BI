Signal discipline

A signal earns attention only after source, stability and explanatory value are tested

Alternative data and machine-assisted research can widen the information set, but weak provenance or unstable relationships should lower confidence before any portfolio implication is considered.

Operating principle

Scale analytical capability only after the decision process is clear

The strongest analytics operating model connects research questions, data, models, review and portfolio decisions, rather than measuring success by the number of models or tools deployed.
