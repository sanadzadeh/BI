Method view
1. Decision questionDefine the service, population, outcome and decision required.

3. ValidationDefinitions, linkage, missingness, outliers and source limitations.
4. AnalysisPython/SQL, descriptive statistics, trends, cohorts, scenarios and sensitivity tests.
